# SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT (SHA)

**THIS SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT** (this "Agreement") is entered into as of June 28, 2026, by and among:
1. **Mosby Tax CRM, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company");
2. **Rick Jefferson**, residing at 1342 NM 333, Tijeras, New Mexico 87059 (the "Lead Founder" or "Majority Stockholder"); and
3. Any other shareholders of the Company who execute a joinder to this Agreement from time to time (each a "Shareholder" and collectively the "Shareholders").

### RECITALS

A. The Shareholders own or are acquiring shares of the Common Stock of the Company.
B. The parties believe that it is in the best interest of the Company and its Shareholders to establish terms regarding the administration, governance, and management of the Company, the voting of shares, and certain exit rights.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the parties hereto agree as follows:

---

## 1. BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND REPRESENTATION

### 1.1 Composition of the Board
Each Shareholder agrees to vote all shares of voting stock owned by such Shareholder, and to take all other necessary actions, to ensure that the Board of Directors of the Company (the "Board") shall consist of three (3) members, designated as follows:
1. **Lead Founder Director**: One (1) director shall be designated by **Rick Jefferson**, who shall initially be **Rick Jefferson** (and who shall serve as the Chairman of the Board);
2. **Co-Founder Director**: One (1) director shall be designated by the Co-Founder; and
3. **Independent Director**: One (1) director, who shall be an industry expert, designated jointly by the other two directors.

### 1.2 Board Meetings and Quorum
- **Frequency**: The Board shall meet at least quarterly, either in person, via teleconference, or by videoconference.
- **Quorum**: A quorum for any meeting of the Board shall require the presence of at least a majority of the directors, including the Lead Founder Director. No resolution of the Board shall be valid unless approved by a majority of the directors present, which majority must include the Lead Founder Director.

---

## 2. PRE-EMPTIVE RIGHTS

### 2.1 Right of Participation
The Company hereby grants to each Shareholder owning at least five percent (5%) of the outstanding capital stock of the Company (a "Major Shareholder") the right of first refusal to purchase its pro-rata share of any New Securities (as defined below) that the Company may, from time to time, propose to sell and issue.
- **"Pro-rata Share"** is defined as the ratio of the number of shares of Common Stock held by such Major Shareholder immediately prior to the issuance of New Securities to the total number of outstanding shares of Common Stock of the Company (on a fully diluted basis).

### 2.2 Definition of New Securities
"New Securities" means any capital stock of the Company, whether now authorized or not, and any rights, options, or warrants to purchase capital stock, and securities convertible into or exchangeable for capital stock. *Excepted from this definition are*:
1. Shares issued to employees, officers, or directors pursuant to any stock option plan or equity incentive plan approved by the Board;
2. Shares issued in connection with any stock split, stock dividend, or recapitalization;
3. Shares issued pursuant to any acquisition, merger, or corporate partnering agreement approved by the Board; and
4. Shares issued in connection with a public offering of the Company's securities.

---

## 3. DRAG-ALONG AND TAG-ALONG RIGHTS

### 3.1 Drag-Along Rights (Majority Drag)
If the Board and the holders of at least sixty percent (60%) of the outstanding Common Stock (including the Lead Founder) approve a transaction that results in a Change of Control of the Company (whether by merger, sale of stock, or sale of substantially all assets) to an unaffiliated third party (an "Approved Sale"), then all Shareholders shall:
1. Consent to, vote in favor of, and raise no objections to the Approved Sale;
2. If the Approved Sale is structured as a sale of stock, agree to sell all of their shares of capital stock on the same terms and conditions as the approving majority; and
3. Execute all customary transaction documents, representations, warranties, and indemnities required to close the sale.

### 3.2 Tag-Along Rights (Co-Sale Rights)
If any Major Shareholder (the "Selling Shareholder") proposes to sell or transfer more than twenty percent (20%) of their outstanding shares to an unaffiliated third party in a single transaction or series of related transactions, the other Shareholders (the "Tagging Shareholders") shall have the right to participate in such sale.
- Each Tagging Shareholder may elect to sell a number of shares up to their pro-rata portion of the shares being sold, calculated as the ratio of the shares owned by the Tagging Shareholder to the aggregate number of shares owned by all participating Shareholders.
- The shares sold by the Tagging Shareholders shall be purchased on the same terms, price, and conditions as the shares sold by the Selling Shareholder.

---

## 4. INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS

### 4.1 Delivery of Financial Statements
The Company shall deliver to each Major Shareholder:
1. **Annual Financials**: Within ninety (90) days after the end of each fiscal year, an unaudited balance sheet and income statement of the Company for such fiscal year;
2. **Quarterly Financials**: Within forty-five (45) days after the end of each fiscal quarter, an unaudited balance sheet and income statement of the Company for such quarter; and
3. **Annual Budget**: At least thirty (30) days prior to the beginning of each fiscal year, a detailed operating budget and business plan for the upcoming fiscal year.

### 4.2 Right of Inspection
Each Major Shareholder shall have the right, at its own expense, to visit and inspect the Company’s properties, examine its books of account and records, and discuss its affairs, finances, and accounts with its officers, during normal business hours and upon reasonable advance notice.

---

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall terminate upon the occurrence of any of the following events:
1. The mutual written agreement of the Company and the Shareholders holding at least eighty percent (80%) of the outstanding Common Stock;
2. The closing of the Company’s initial public offering (IPO) registered under the Securities Act of 1933; or
3. The dissolution or liquidation of the Company.

---

## 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 6.1 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the **State of Delaware**, without giving effect to conflict of laws principles.

### 6.2 Dispute Resolution
All disputes, controversies, or claims arising out of this Agreement shall be settled by confidential binding arbitration administered by the **American Arbitration Association (AAA)** in **Albuquerque, New Mexico**, in accordance with its Commercial Arbitration Rules.

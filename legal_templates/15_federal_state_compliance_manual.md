## 1. DELAWARE STATE CORPORATE COMPLIANCE

As a Delaware C-Corporation, Mosby Tax CRM, Inc. must maintain its corporate charter in good standing with the State of Delaware, Division of Corporations.

### 1.1 Delaware Annual Franchise Tax & Annual Report
* **Requirement**: All active corporations incorporated in Delaware must file an Annual Franchise Tax Report and pay the associated franchise tax.
* **Due Date**: **March 1st of every calendar year** (non-negotiable).
* **Methods of Calculation**:
  1. **Authorized Shares Method**: Based on the total number of authorized shares (10,000,000 shares for Mosby Tax CRM, Inc.). *Minimum tax is $250,000 using this method, which is highly cost-inefficient for startups.*
  2. **Assumed Par Value Capital Method (RECOMMENDED)**: Based on total gross assets and total issued shares. This method typically reduces the annual franchise tax to the minimum range of **$400.00 to $500.00** for early-stage companies.
* **Annual Report Fee**: $50.00 filing fee (paid at the time of Franchise Tax submission).
* **Penalty for Late Filing**: Immediate $200.00 penalty plus interest at 1.5% per month.

### 1.2 Registered Agent Maintenance
* **Requirement**: The Corporation must maintain a registered agent with a physical street address in Delaware at all times.
* **Provider**: National Registered Agents, Inc. (Wilmington, DE).
* **Annual Cost**: Approximately $150.00 to $300.00, billed annually. Any changes to the registered agent must be formally filed with the Delaware Secretary of State.

## 2. FEDERAL TAX & IRS COMPLIANCE

Mosby Tax CRM, Inc. must register with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and maintain federal compliance in accordance with the Internal Revenue Code (IRC).

### 2.1 Employer Identification Number (EIN)
* **Status**: Obtained immediately upon incorporation by filing **Form SS-4**.
* **Purpose**: Required for opening corporate bank accounts, establishing payroll, registering with state tax offices, and filing tax returns.

### 2.2 Corporate Income Tax (Form 1120)
* **Requirement**: All Delaware C-Corporations must file an annual federal income tax return on **Form 1120**, regardless of whether the company generated revenue or turned a profit.
* **Tax Rate**: Flat **21%** federal corporate tax rate on net taxable income.
* **Due Date**: **April 15th of each year** (for calendar year corporations), or the 15th day of the 4th month following the close of the fiscal year.
* **Extension**: Eligible to file for an automatic 6-month extension using **Form 7004** (moving the filing deadline to October 15th).

### 2.3 Information Returns (W-2 and 1099)
* **Form W-2 (Wage and Tax Statement)**: Must be filed annually for each employee. A copy must be provided to the employee and submitted to the Social Security Administration (SSA) by **January 31st**.
* **Form 1099-NEC (Nonemployee Compensation)**: Must be filed for each independent contractor paid **$600.00 or more** in a calendar year. Must be filed and provided to the contractor by **January 31st**.

### 2.4 Section 83(b) Election Audits
* **Action**: Ensure that every founder and early employee receiving unvested Common Stock completes and submits an **IRC Section 83(b) Election** to the IRS within **thirty (30) days** of their stock purchase.
* **Fiduciary Audit**: Keep a scanned copy of the stamped, certified mail receipt of the 83(b) filing in the Company’s permanent corporate records, as this is critical for future investor due diligence.

## 3. STATE OF NEW MEXICO OPERATIONAL COMPLIANCE

Since Mosby Tax CRM, Inc.'s primary operational office and founder, Rick Jefferson, are based in Tijeras, New Mexico, the Corporation must register as a foreign entity in New Mexico and comply with all state-level taxes and regulations.

### 3.1 Foreign Corporation Qualification
* **Requirement**: File an Application for Certificate of Authority with the **New Mexico Secretary of State** to qualify to do business as a foreign Delaware corporation in New Mexico.
* **Fee**: Approximately $200.00.
* **Annual Report**: Must file a New Mexico corporate annual report every two years, with a $25.00 filing fee.

### 3.2 State Taxes (New Mexico Taxation & Revenue Department)
1. **Corporate Income Tax**: New Mexico corporate income tax rates range from 4.8% to 5.9% of net income allocated to the state. Filed on **Form CIT-1**.
2. **Gross Receipts Tax (GRT)**: New Mexico does not have a standard sales tax; instead, it imposes a Gross Receipts Tax on businesses for the privilege of doing business in New Mexico. 
   - **Rate**: Billed on gross receipts from software services, consulting, or subscriptions sold to New Mexico residents. Early-stage SaaS sales to out-of-state users may qualify for a GRT deduction (interstate services).
   - **Filing Frequency**: Monthly, quarterly, or semi-annually based on average tax liability.
3. **Employer Withholding and Unemployment Insurance (SUI)**:
   - Must register for state income tax withholding and State Unemployment Insurance (SUI) with the New Mexico Department of Workforce Solutions.
   - SUI rates are adjusted annually based on the company's employment history and claims.

## 5. CORPORATE GOVERNANCE AND RECORD MAINTENANCE

To protect the corporate veil and limit personal liability, the Corporation must strictly maintain its corporate records.

### 5.1 Annual Stockholders and Board Meetings
* **Action**: Hold an annual meeting of the stockholders to elect the Board of Directors, and an annual meeting of the Board of Directors to elect corporate officers.
* **Minutes**: Draft detailed written **Minutes** of all stockholder and Board meetings, which must be approved by the Secretary and filed in the corporate minute book.

### 5.2 Written Consents
* **Action**: Minor Board actions may be authorized without a formal meeting by executing a **Unanimous Written Consent (UWC)** signed by all directors of the Corporation.

### 5.3 Corporate Minute Book Contents
The corporate Secretary shall maintain a secure physical and digital Minute Book containing:
1. Delaware Certificate of Incorporation (including file-stamped copy);
2. NM Certificate of Authority (for foreign qualification);
3. Bylaws of Mosby Tax CRM, Inc. (duly adopted);
4. Signed Founder Collaboration and Stock Purchase Agreements;
5. Executed Section 83(b) Elections;
6. Up-to-date Cap Table Ledger;
7. Minutes of all board and stockholder meetings, or Unanimous Written Consents; and
8. Signed PIIA agreements from all employees and contractors.

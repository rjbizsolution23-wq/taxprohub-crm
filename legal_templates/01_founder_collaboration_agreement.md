# FOUNDER COLLABORATION & EQUITY VESTING AGREEMENT

**THIS FOUNDER COLLABORATION & EQUITY VESTING AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of June 28, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between the undersigned founding individuals (each a "Founder" and collectively the "Founders") for the purpose of outlining the terms of their collaboration prior to and in anticipation of the incorporation of a Delaware C-Corporation (the "Company").

### RECITALS

A. The Founders wish to collaborate on the development of a business idea, software platform, and commercial enterprise related to tax CRM, SaaS workflows, and financial systems (the "Project").
B. The Founders intend to incorporate the Company in the State of Delaware to carry on the Project as a formal corporate entity.
C. The Founders wish to define their respective rights, duties, and equity ownership percentages, and to establish a vesting schedule for their stock to ensure long-term alignment and commitment to the Company.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Founders agree as follows:

---

## 1. PRE-INCORPORATION COOPERATIVE EFFORTS

### 1.1 Scope of Collaboration
Each Founder shall contribute their skills, knowledge, and experience to the development of the Project. The initial roles and responsibilities are designated as follows:
- **Rick Jefferson**: Chief Executive Officer (CEO) & Product Architect. Lead overall corporate strategy, product vision, commercial execution, and product-market fit.
- **Co-Founder**: Chief Technology Officer (CTO). Lead software engineering, backend systems, database architecture, and technology infrastructure.

### 1.2 Devotion of Time
Each Founder agrees to commit their full-time business attention, effort, and professional skills to the Project, working diligently toward the incorporation and commercial success of the Company.

## 3. EQUITY VESTING AND REPURCHASE RIGHT

To ensure the continuous dedication of the Founders to the Project and the Company, all Common Stock issued to the Founders shall be subject to a right of repurchase by the Company (the "Repurchase Right") under the following vesting terms:

### 3.1 Vesting Schedule
Except as otherwise provided herein, the Common Stock issued to each Founder shall vest over a four (4) year period in accordance with the following schedule, commencing on the Effective Date (the "Vesting Commencement Date"):
- **One-Year Cliff**: Twenty-five percent (25%) of the allocated shares shall vest on the one (1) year anniversary of the Vesting Commencement Date (June 28, 2027), provided the Founder remains in continuous service as an employee, consultant, or director of the Company.
- **Monthly Vesting**: The remaining seventy-five percent (75%) of the shares shall vest in equal monthly installments over the subsequent thirty-six (36) months on the last day of each calendar month, provided the Founder remains in continuous service to the Company.

### 3.2 Repurchase Right upon Termination
In the event that a Founder’s service to the Project or the Company terminates for any reason (including voluntary resignation, death, disability, or termination with or without Cause), the Company (or the remaining Founders if incorporation is pending) shall have an irrevocable option to repurchase any unvested shares from the departing Founder at the lower of:
1. The original purchase price per share paid by the Founder; or
2. The fair market value of the unvested shares as determined in good faith by the Board of Directors.

### 3.3 Accelerated Vesting
- **Single-Trigger Acceleration**: In the event of a Change of Control of the Company (such as an acquisition, merger, or sale of substantially all assets), fifty percent (50%) of each Founder’s then-unvested shares shall immediately vest.
- **Double-Trigger Acceleration**: In the event that a Founder is terminated without "Cause" or resigns for "Good Reason" within twelve (12) months following a Change of Control, one hundred percent (100%) of their remaining unvested shares shall immediately vest.

### 3.4 Section 83(b) Election
Each Founder acknowledges that upon the purchase or issuance of unvested Common Stock, they may face significant tax consequences. Each Founder agrees to consult with their own tax advisor regarding the advisability of filing an election under **Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the "IRC")**, within thirty (30) days of the stock issuance date, and to provide a copy of such filed election to the Company.

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY REPRESENTATION & ASSIGNMENT

### 4.1 Prior Work and Contribution
Each Founder represents and warrants that all ideas, algorithms, business plans, software, websites, source code, workflows, designs, patents, trademarks, or trade secrets developed by them, either individually or jointly, in connection with the Project prior to the Effective Date are free of any third-party claims, liens, or encumbrances.

### 4.2 Assignment of IP upon Incorporation
Each Founder hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company (or covenants to assign to the Company immediately upon its formal incorporation) all of their right, title, and interest in and to any and all intellectual property, work product, software, designs, algorithms, domain names, and database systems created or conceived during the pre-incorporation collaboration. No Founder shall retain any individual ownership rights in the Project IP.

## 5. ROLES AND CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

### 5.1 Board of Directors
Upon incorporation, the initial Board of Directors of the Company shall consist of two (2) members:
1. **Rick Jefferson** (Chairman of the Board)
2. **Co-Founder**

### 5.2 Key Decisions Requiring Unanimous Approval
Prior to the closing of a qualified equity financing round of at least $1,000,000, the following corporate actions shall require the unanimous consent of the Board of Directors:
1. Any amendment to the Certificate of Incorporation or Bylaws.
2. The issuance of any debt instruments or lines of credit exceeding $25,000.
3. The sale, lease, license, or transfer of substantially all of the Company's assets.
4. Any increase or decrease in the size of the Board of Directors.
5. The creation of any new class of stock or equity derivative.

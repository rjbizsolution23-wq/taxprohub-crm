## 2. RIGHTS, PREFERENCES, AND PRIVILEGES OF PREFERRED SHARES

### 2.1 Liquidation Preference
In the event of any liquidation, dissolution, or winding up of the Company (including a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets):
* The holders of Preferred Shares shall be entitled to receive, in preference to the holders of Common Stock, an amount equal to **one times (1.0x) the Original Purchase Price ($0.50 per share)**, plus any declared but unpaid dividends.
* After the payment of the preferred liquidation preference, any remaining assets of the Company shall be distributed pro-rata among the holders of Common Stock.

### 2.2 Conversion Rights
The Preferred Shares shall be convertible at any time, at the option of the holder, into shares of Common Stock.
* **Initial Conversion Ratio**: 1-to-1 conversion ratio, subject to standard anti-dilution adjustments (for stock splits, dividends, and recapitalizations).
* **Automatic Conversion**: The Preferred Shares shall automatically convert into Common Stock upon:
  1. The closing of a qualified public offering (IPO) on a recognized stock exchange with gross proceeds to the Company of at least $15,000,000; or
  2. The written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the outstanding Preferred Shares.

### 2.3 Voting Rights
The holders of Preferred Shares shall be entitled to vote on all corporate matters on an **as-converted to Common Stock basis**. Except as required by Delaware law or the protective provisions below, the Preferred Shares and Common Stock shall vote together as a single class.

### 2.4 Protective Provisions (Investor Covenants)
So long as any Preferred Shares remain outstanding, the Company shall not take any of the following actions without the affirmative vote or written consent of the holders of at least a majority of the outstanding Preferred Shares:
1. Liquidate, dissolve, wind up, or merge the Company;
2. Amend, alter, or repeal any provision of the Certificate of Incorporation or Bylaws in a manner that adversely affects the rights of the Preferred Shares;
3. Authorize or issue any new class or series of stock having preferences senior to or on parity with the Preferred Shares;
4. Declare or pay any dividend on Common Stock; or
5. Increase or decrease the authorized number of directors on the Board.

## 4. PRE-EMPTIVE RIGHTS AND RIGHT OF CO-SALE

### 4.1 Right of Participation
Major Investors (holding at least 100,000 Preferred Shares) shall have a right of first refusal to purchase their pro-rata share of any future equity issuances by the Company, so long as they remain shareholders.

### 4.2 Co-Sale and Tag-Along Rights
If any Founder proposes to sell their shares of Common Stock to a third party, the holders of Preferred Shares shall have:
1. A **Right of First Refusal** to purchase their pro-rata portion of the Founder's shares on the same terms; and
2. A **Co-Sale / Tag-Along Right** to participate in the sale on a pro-rata basis on the same terms.

---

## 5. REVENUE BINDING COVENANTS (EXCLUSIVITY & LEGAL STATUS)

### 5.1 Exclusivity Period (BINDING)
For a period of **forty-five (45) days** following the date this Term Sheet is signed by both parties (the "Exclusivity Period"), the Company, its founders, and employees agree not to solicit, encourage, initiate, or engage in any discussions or negotiations with, nor provide any information to, any third party regarding any equity investment, merger, acquisition, or sale of the Company.

### 5.2 Governing Law (BINDING)
This Term Sheet and all actions arising hereunder shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the **State of Delaware**, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Any legal proceeding arising under this Term Sheet shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in **Albuquerque, New Mexico**.

### 5.3 Expenses
The Company and the Investors shall each bear their own legal and operational expenses incurred in connection with the negotiation and closing of this transaction.

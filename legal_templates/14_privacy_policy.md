# PRIVACY POLICY
# MOSBY TAX CRM PLATFORM

*Last Updated: June 28, 2026*

At **Mosby Tax CRM**, we are deeply committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of the personal and financial information of our users, clients, and partners. 

This Privacy Policy describes how **Mosby Tax CRM, Inc.** ("Mosby Tax CRM", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects your information when you visit our website, register an account, or use the Mosby Tax CRM platform (the "Service").

---

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 1.1 Personal Data You Provide
We collect information that you voluntarily provide when creating an account, subscribing to our services, or interacting with our customer support, including:
* **Contact Information**: Name, company name, professional email address, phone number, and physical mailing address.
* **Credentials**: Usernames, passwords, multi-factor authentication (MFA) parameters, and API keys.
* **Billing Information**: Credit card details, billing address, bank account numbers, or Stripe customer identifiers (all processed securely through PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways).

### 1.2 Customer and Taxpayer Data
When using our SaaS platform to manage your business operations and client tax files, you may upload, import, or enter data about your own clients (the "Taxpayer Data"), which may include:
* Full names, Social Security Numbers (SSNs), Employer Identification Numbers (EINs), wage statements (W-2s, 1099s), tax returns, and asset ledgers.
* **Strict Statutory Safeguard**: We treat all Taxpayer Data in accordance with the stringent requirements of **Section 7216 of the United States Internal Revenue Code** and standard IRS security directives. We do not use or disclose this data for any purpose other than providing the CRM and automation services you have explicitly requested.

### 1.3 Automated and Technical Information
When you access our platform, our servers automatically record certain technical and usage information, including:
* IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referral URLs, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits.
* **Cookies and Tracking**: We use essential and functional cookies, web beacons, and tracking pixels to preserve your session state, authenticate your logins, and monitor platform performance. You can manage your cookie preferences through your web browser settings.

---

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
We process your information for the following business purposes:
1. **Providing and Maintaining the Service**: Processing transactions, maintaining your CRM databases, routing automated workflows, and managing user access.
2. **Customer Support**: Responding to support tickets, resolving software bugs, and troubleshooting database issues.
3. **Billing and Subscription Management**: Processing recurring payments via Stripe, managing renewal terms, and delivering invoices.
4. **Platform Security**: Preventing unauthorized access, defending against DDoS attacks, auditing access logs, and verifying multi-factor authentication.
5. **Partner Tracking**: Calculating, auditing, and processing partner payouts and referral commissions.
6. **Regulatory Compliance**: Meeting our federal, state, and local legal obligations, including tax filings and records retention.

---

## 3. HOW WE SHARE AND DISCLOSE INFORMATION
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or taxpayer data. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:
1. **Third-Party Service Providers**: We share data with trusted third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf and are bound by strict confidentiality agreements, such as:
   - **Payment Processing**: Stripe, Inc.
   - **Hosting & Infrastructure**: Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare Workers, and Supabase.
   - **Transactional Email & Notifications**: Resend, Inc.
2. **Legal and Regulatory Authorities**: We may disclose information if required to do so by a valid subpoena, court order, or search warrant, or to comply with IRS or state department of revenue audits.
3. **Corporate Transitions**: In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or a portion of our corporate assets, customer data may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to the confidentiality terms of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 4. DATA SECURITY AND CYBERSECURITY STANDARDS
Mosby Tax CRM employs enterprise-grade administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your data:
* **Encryption**: All data transmitted to or from our platform is encrypted in transit using **Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3)**. All data stored in our databases is encrypted at rest using **Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256)**.
* **Access Control**: Internal access to our production databases and servers is highly restricted and requires multi-factor authentication, hardware security keys, and formal audit logs.
* **Vulnerability Scanning**: We conduct regular security audits, synthetic user probes, and code vulnerability scans to detect and remediate security risks.

## 5. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

### 5.1 California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)
If you are a California resident, you are entitled to the following rights under the CCPA/CPRA:
* **Right to Know**: The right to request that we disclose what personal information we collect, use, and share about you.
* **Right to Delete**: The right to request the deletion of your personal information collected or maintained by us, subject to standard legal exceptions.
* **Right to Correct**: The right to request that we correct inaccurate personal information that we maintain about you.
* **Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing**: The right to opt-out of any "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information (as defined by CCPA). Mosby Tax CRM does not sell or share your personal information.
* **Right to Non-Discrimination**: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA/CPRA rights.

### 5.2 European Union Data Protection Rights (GDPR)
If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the GDPR:
* The right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data.
* The right to restrict or object to our processing of your personal data.
* The right to data portability, allowing you to obtain and reuse your personal data across different services.
* The right to withdraw consent at any time, where our processing is based on your consent.
* To exercise these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at support@rjbusinesssolutions.org.

When data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with industry-standard sanitation procedures.

# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

1. **Mosby Tax CRM, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation, having its principal place of business at 1342 NM 333, Tijeras, New Mexico 87059 ("Mosby Tax CRM"); and
2. **[Company Name]**, a **[State of Incorporation]** corporation, having its principal office at **[Partner Business Address]** (the "Counterparty").

Each party may be referred to herein individually as a "Party" or collectively as the "Parties".

### RECITALS

A. The Parties are exploring a potential business relationship or transaction of mutual interest, including but not limited to partnership, API integration, tax CRM utilization, or service delivery (the "Purpose").
B. In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other Party certain proprietary, non-public, and highly confidential business, technical, financial, or tax-processing information.
C. The Parties wish to establish terms to govern the use, protection, and non-disclosure of such information.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

### 1.1 Scope
"Confidential Information" means any and all information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally, in writing, or in electronic or other form, that is marked or identified as confidential or proprietary, or that by its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure should reasonably be understood to be confidential. 

### 1.2 Inclusions
Confidential Information includes, without limitation:
1. **Technical Data**: Software, source code, database architectures, APIs, algorithms, designs, schemas, product roadmaps, and technical specifications related to Mosby Tax CRM;
2. **Business Data**: Business plans, marketing strategies, customer lists, prospect databases, pricing models, financials, and sales funnels; and
3. **Sensitive Client Data**: Any tax records, tax return data, taxpayer identification numbers (SSNs or EINs), financial statements, or PII (Personally Identifiable Information) processed through the systems of either Party.

---

## 2. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential Information does not include any information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by written evidence:
1. Was already in the public domain at the time of disclosure, or subsequently became part of the public domain through no fault of the Receiving Party;
2. Was in the lawful possession of the Receiving Party prior to its disclosure by the Disclosing Party without restriction on disclosure;
3. Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
4. Was received from a third party who had a lawful right to disclose such information without breaching any confidentiality obligations.

---

## 3. NON-USE AND NON-DISCLOSURE OBLIGATIONS

### 3.1 Protection Standards
The Receiving Party shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall use at least the same degree of care to protect it as it uses to protect its own highly sensitive confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable standard of care.

### 3.2 Restrictive Use
The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party solely for the Purpose described in this Agreement and for no other commercial or personal purpose.

### 3.3 Limited Access
The Receiving Party shall limit access to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to those of its employees, officers, legal counsel, and financial advisors who:
1. Have a strict "need to know" such information to fulfill the Purpose; and
2. Are bound by written confidentiality agreements or professional ethical duties at least as restrictive as the terms of this Agreement.

---

## 4. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE
If the Receiving Party is requested or required by law, subpoena, court order, or regulatory agency (such as the Internal Revenue Service or state tax departments) to disclose any of the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall:
1. To the extent legally permitted, provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice of such requirement so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or contest the disclosure; and
2. Co-operate fully with the Disclosing Party, at the Disclosing Party's expense, in seeking such protection.
If no protective order is obtained, the Receiving Party shall disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that it is legally advised it is compelled to disclose, and shall request confidential treatment for any information so disclosed.

---

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 5.1 Agreement Duration
This Agreement shall continue in full force and effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date, unless terminated earlier by either Party upon thirty (30) days’ written notice.

### 5.2 Survival of Obligations
The confidentiality and non-use obligations set forth in this Agreement shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years from the date of disclosure; *provided, however*, that with respect to trade secrets, source code, or proprietary algorithms, the obligations shall survive indefinitely or for as long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable US law.

---

## 6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF INFORMATION
Upon the written request of the Disclosing Party, or upon the termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall, at the Disclosing Party's option, immediately return or destroy all physical or electronic documents, files, and materials containing or representing any Confidential Information, and certify such destruction in writing within ten (10) business days. 

---

## 7. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

### 7.1 No License or Warranty
No license, copyright, patent, trademark, or other intellectual property right is granted or implied by the disclosure of Confidential Information under this Agreement. All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS", without any express or implied warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose.

### 7.2 Injunctive Relief
The Receiving Party acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the Disclosing Party for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Therefore, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek temporary, preliminary, and permanent injunctive relief to prevent or restrain any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement, without the necessity of posting a bond.

### 7.3 Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the **State of Delaware**, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. Any legal action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in **Albuquerque, New Mexico** (as the closest federal jurisdiction to the principal founder operations).

### 7.4 Successors and Assigns
This Agreement shall bind and inure to the benefit of the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

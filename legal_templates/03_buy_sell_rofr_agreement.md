# BUY-SELL AGREEMENT & RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL (ROFR)

**THIS BUY-SELL & RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is entered into as of June 28, 2026, by and among **Mosby Tax CRM, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and **Rick Jefferson** and any other stockholders of the Company who become signatories hereto (each a "Stockholder" and collectively the "Stockholders").

### RECITALS

A. The Stockholders are the owners of shares of the Common Stock of the Company.
B. The Stockholders and the Company believe that it is in their best interest to promote stability in the ownership and management of the Company and to avoid the transfer of shares to persons whose interests might be inconsistent with those of the Company or its current management.
C. Therefore, the parties desire to restrict the sale, transfer, assignment, pledge, or other disposition of shares of the Company's Common Stock, and to provide for certain repurchase options and rights of first refusal.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. RESTRICTIONS ON TRANSFER

### 1.1 General Limitation
Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, no Stockholder shall sell, assign, transfer, pledge, hypothecate, gift, or in any manner dispose of or encumber any shares of Common Stock of the Company (or any interest therein) now owned or hereafter acquired by such Stockholder (the "Shares") without the prior written consent of the Company and compliance with the procedures set forth herein.

### 1.2 Permitted Transfers
The restrictions in this Agreement shall not apply to transfers made:
1. To the Stockholder’s spouse, children, or a trust established solely for the benefit of the Stockholder or their immediate family members for estate planning purposes;
2. To another existing Stockholder of the Company; or
3. To the Company pursuant to any repurchase or vesting agreements.
*Provided, however*, that any such permitted transferee shall execute a joinder agreement agreeing to be bound by all the terms, conditions, and restrictions of this Agreement.

---

## 2. RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL (ROFR)

### 2.1 Notice of Proposed Transfer
In the event that a Stockholder (the "Transferring Stockholder") receives a bona fide, written offer from a third party (the "Third-Party Offeror") to purchase any of their Shares, and the Transferring Stockholder desires to accept such offer, they shall deliver a written notice (the "Transfer Notice") to the Company and the other Stockholders (the "Remaining Stockholders") stating:
1. The number of Shares proposed to be transferred (the "Offered Shares");
2. The name and address of the Third-Party Offeror;
3. The proposed purchase price per Share; and
4. All other material terms and conditions of the proposed transfer.

### 2.2 Company's Option to Purchase
For a period of thirty (30) days following receipt of the Transfer Notice (the "Company Option Period"), the Company shall have an irrevocable option to purchase all or any portion of the Offered Shares at the price and upon the terms specified in the Transfer Notice. The Company shall exercise this option by delivering written notice to the Transferring Stockholder prior to the expiration of the Company Option Period.

### 2.3 Stockholders' Option to Purchase
If the Company does not elect to purchase all of the Offered Shares, the Company shall, within five (5) days after the expiration of the Company Option Period, notify the Remaining Stockholders of the remaining Offered Shares. 
- For a period of fifteen (15) days following such notice (the "Stockholder Option Period"), each Remaining Stockholder shall have the option to purchase their pro-rata share of the remaining Offered Shares.
- "Pro-rata" shall be calculated as the ratio of the shares owned by a Remaining Stockholder to the total number of shares owned by all Remaining Stockholders.
- If any Remaining Stockholder declines to purchase their full pro-rata share, the other Remaining Stockholders shall have the right to purchase the unsubscribed shares (an "Over-Allotment Option").

### 2.4 Right to Sell to Third Party
If the Company and the Remaining Stockholders do not elect to purchase all of the Offered Shares, the Transferring Stockholder may sell the remaining unpurchased Offered Shares to the Third-Party Offeror, but only:
1. Within sixty (60) days following the expiration of the Stockholder Option Period;
2. At a price not less than that specified in the Transfer Notice; and
3. Upon terms and conditions no more favorable to the Third-Party Offeror than those set forth in the Transfer Notice.
Any transferee must execute a joinder to this Agreement prior to and as a condition of the registration of the stock transfer on the books of the Company.

---

## 3. INVOLUNTARY TRANSFERS AND BUY-SELL TRIGGERS

### 3.1 Triggering Events
The Company shall have the option to purchase all Shares owned by a Stockholder upon the occurrence of any of the following events (each a "Triggering Event"):
1. The death of the Stockholder;
2. The permanent disability of the Stockholder (defined as the inability to perform material duties for 120 consecutive days or 180 days in any 12-month period);
3. The filing of a voluntary or involuntary petition in bankruptcy by or against the Stockholder; or
4. The termination of the Stockholder’s employment or service relationship with the Company for Cause.

### 3.2 Valuation of Shares
For the purposes of a repurchase following a Triggering Event under Section 3.1, the purchase price per Share shall be determined as follows:
- The Fair Market Value (FMV) of the Shares shall be established by mutual agreement of the Board of Directors and the Stockholder (or their legal representative) in good faith.
- If no agreement can be reached within thirty (30) days of the Triggering Event, the FMV shall be determined by an independent qualified business appraiser appointed by mutual agreement, whose expenses shall be shared equally by the Company and the Stockholder (or their estate).

### 3.3 Terms of Repurchase
The Company may pay the purchase price determined under Section 3.2:
1. In cash within ninety (90) days of the valuation; or
2. By delivering a promissory note payable in equal consecutive quarterly installments over a period not to exceed five (5) years, bearing interest at the Prime Rate published in the Wall Street Journal on the date of issuance.

---

## 4. RESTRICTIVE LEGENDS
Each certificate representing shares of capital stock of the Company now owned or hereafter acquired by any Stockholder shall have endorsed on its face or back a legend in substantially the following form:

> "THE SHARES REPRESENTED BY THIS CERTIFICATE ARE SUBJECT TO THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF A BUY-SELL & RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL AGREEMENT DATED JUNE 28, 2026, WHICH CONTAINS RESTRICTIONS ON SALE, TRANSFER, PLEDGE, OR OTHER DISPOSITION. A COPY OF THE AGREEMENT IS ON FILE AT THE PRINCIPAL OFFICE OF THE CORPORATION AND WILL BE FURNISHED TO THE HOLDER THEREOF WITHOUT CHARGE UPON WRITTEN REQUEST."

## 2. HARASSMENT-FREE WORKPLACE & REPORTING PROCEDURES

### 2.1 Anti-Harassment Policy
The Company is dedicated to providing a work environment free of harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. Harassment of any kind, whether based on sex, race, religion, age, or any other protected characteristic, will not be tolerated. This policy applies to all employees, independent contractors, clients, and partners of the Company.

### 2.2 Sexual Harassment Definition
In accordance with federal and state regulations, sexual harassment includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal, physical, or visual conduct of a sexual nature when:
1. Submission to such conduct is made an explicit or implicit term of employment;
2. Submission to or rejection of such conduct is used as the basis for employment decisions; or
3. Such conduct unreasonably interferes with an individual's work performance or creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive working environment.

### 2.3 Complaint Resolution Process

## 3. FAMILY AND MEDICAL LEAVE ACT (FMLA) SUMMARY
Under the federal **Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)**, eligible employees are entitled to take up to twelve (12) weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for specified family and medical reasons, including:
1. The birth of a child, or placement of a child with the employee for adoption or foster care;
2. To care for an immediate family member (spouse, child, or parent) with a serious health condition; or
3. A serious health condition that makes the employee unable to perform the essential functions of their job.
*Note*: FMLA applies to employees who have worked for the Company for at least twelve (12) months and have accumulated at least 1,250 hours of service, at a location where the Company employs fifty (50) or more employees within a seventy-five (75) mile radius.

## 4. HOURS OF WORK & OVERTIME POLICIES (FLSA)

### 4.1 Work Hours and Classifications
- **Regular Hours**: The standard workweek for full-time employees is forty (40) hours, typically Monday through Friday.
- **Exempt Employees**: Employees classified as exempt under the **Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)** are paid a fixed salary and are not eligible for overtime compensation.
- **Non-Exempt Employees**: Employees classified as non-exempt are paid hourly and are eligible to receive overtime pay at a rate of **one and a half times (1.5x)** their regular hourly rate for all hours worked in excess of forty (40) hours in a single workweek.

### 4.2 Mandatory Time Recording
All non-exempt employees must accurately record their daily hours worked using the Company's designated time tracking system. Altering, falsifying, or failing to record hours worked is a material violation of Company policy.

## 5. CYBERSECURITY, DATA PRIVACY, AND CLIENT CONFIDENTIALITY

### 5.1 Protecting Taxpayer PII
As a tax CRM software company, Mosby Tax CRM, Inc. handles highly sensitive financial and tax-processing records. Every employee has a strict fiduciary and legal duty to protect the privacy of this data.
* **Internal Revenue Code Section 7216**: It is a federal crime to disclose or use any tax return information without the express written consent of the taxpayer. Violation of this code carries criminal penalties, including fines and imprisonment.

### 5.2 Security Protocols
1. **Password Integrity**: Employees must use strong, unique passwords and enable **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** on all Company devices, email, and CRM database accounts.
2. **Clean Desk & Clean Screen**: Employees must lock their computer screens whenever leaving their desks or workspaces.
3. **No Unapproved Software**: Employees shall not download, install, or use any third-party software, browser extensions, or cloud storage accounts on Company computers without the written authorization of the CTO.

## PART I: PLAN RULES & REGULATIONS

### 1. PURPOSE OF THE PLAN
The purpose of the Mosby Tax CRM, Inc. 2026 Equity Incentive Plan (the "Plan") is to secure and retain the services of employees, directors, and consultants of Mosby Tax CRM, Inc. (the "Company") and its affiliates, to provide incentives for such persons to exert maximum efforts for the success of the Company, and to provide a means by which they may benefit from increases in the value of the Common Stock of the Company.

### 2. SHARES SUBJECT TO THE PLAN
The maximum number of shares of Common Stock that may be issued under the Plan is **1,500,000 shares**, subject to adjustment in the event of stock splits, stock dividends, reorganizations, or similar recapitalizations. Shares subject to options that expire, expire unexercised, or are forfeited shall return to the Plan pool and become available for future grant.

### 3. ADMINISTRATION OF THE PLAN
The Plan shall be administered by the Board of Directors of the Company (the "Administrator"). The Administrator shall have the sole power and authority to:
1. Determine the eligibility of employees, consultants, and directors to receive options;
2. Determine the number of shares to be covered by each option;
3. Determine the type of option (Incentive Stock Options under Section 422 of the Internal Revenue Code, or Non-statutory Stock Options);
4. Determine the strike price, vesting schedule, and expiration date of each option; and
5. Interpret the Plan and draft any rules, regulations, and agreements required for its implementation.

### 4. ELIGIBILITY
- **Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)** may be granted only to employees (including officers and directors who are also employees) of the Company.
- **Non-statutory Stock Options (NSOs)** may be granted to employees, directors, and independent consultants of the Company.

### 5. STRIKE PRICE (FAIR MARKET VALUE)
The strike price per share of an option shall be determined by the Administrator on the date of grant. 
- For NSOs and ISOs, the strike price shall not be less than **one hundred percent (100%)** of the Fair Market Value (FMV) of the Common Stock on the date of grant, determined in accordance with **Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code**.
- For ISOs granted to any employee who owns stock representing more than ten percent (10%) of the total voting power of all classes of stock of the Company, the strike price shall not be less than **one hundred ten percent (110%)** of the FMV on the date of grant.

### 6. TERM AND EXPIRATION OF OPTIONS
Options granted under this Plan shall expire no later than **ten (10) years** from the date of grant (or five (5) years for a 10% stockholder holding an ISO). Upon termination of the optionee's service relationship with the Company:
- **Unvested Options** shall immediately terminate and be forfeited.
- **Vested Options** may be exercised within ninety (90) days following termination (unless termination is for Cause, in which case all options, whether vested or unvested, shall immediately terminate).

# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

**THIS EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is entered into as of June 28, 2026, by and between **Mosby Tax CRM, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and **Rick Jefferson**, an individual residing at 1342 NM 333, Tijeras, New Mexico 87059 (the "Executive").

### RECITALS

A. The Company develops, sells, and maintains specialized tax CRM databases, financial workflow automations, and related customer relationship software systems.
B. The Company desires to employ the Executive as its Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and President, and the Executive desires to accept such employment, upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.
C. The Company and the Executive intend for this Agreement to comply with all applicable United States federal, state, and local laws, including Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), and Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. POSITION, DUTIES, AND TERM

### 1.1 Employment Title and Role
The Company hereby employs the Executive as its **Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and President**. The Executive shall report directly to the Board of Directors of the Company (the "Board").

### 1.2 Duties and Authority
The Executive shall have the standard duties, responsibilities, and authority associated with the position of CEO of a software corporation under the Delaware General Corporation Law. The Executive shall lead the Company's strategic vision, software product architecture, hiring, capital raising, and day-to-day operations.

### 1.3 Best Efforts and Devotion of Time
The Executive agrees to devote his full business time, energy, and attention to the performance of his duties under this Agreement. The Executive shall perform his duties faithfully and to the best of his abilities.

### 1.4 At-Will Employment Relationship
The Executive's employment with the Company is **"at-will"**, meaning that either the Company or the Executive may terminate the employment relationship at any time, for any reason or no reason, with or without Cause, subject to the severance provisions set forth in Section 4 of this Agreement.

---

## 2. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

### 2.1 Base Salary
In consideration for his services, the Company shall pay the Executive an initial annual base salary of **$180,000.00** (the "Base Salary"), payable in equal semi-monthly installments in accordance with the Company’s standard payroll practices, subject to all standard federal, state, and local tax withholdings. The Base Salary shall be reviewed annually by the Board and may be increased (but not decreased) at the Board's discretion.

### 2.2 Performance Bonus
The Executive shall be eligible to receive an annual discretionary performance bonus with a target of fifty percent (50%) of his Base Salary. The actual bonus paid shall be determined by the Board (or its Compensation Committee) based on the achievement of specific financial and operational milestones (such as ARR, client acquisition, and database uptime targets).

### 2.3 Executive Benefits
The Executive shall be entitled to participate in all employee benefit plans and programs made available by the Company to its senior executives, including:
1. **Health Insurance**: Medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage (with the Company paying 100% of the premium for the Executive and his dependents);
2. **Retirement Plan**: Participation in the Company’s 401(k) retirement savings plan, including any matching contributions authorized by the Board; and
3. **Paid Time Off (PTO)**: Twenty-five (25) business days of paid vacation per year, in addition to standard federal holidays.

---

## 3. BUSINESS EXPENSES
The Company shall reimburse the Executive for all reasonable, ordinary, and necessary business expenses incurred by him in the performance of his duties, including travel, client entertainment, cell phone/internet services, and professional association fees, upon submission of proper documentation and expense reports in accordance with the Company’s reimbursement policies.

---

## 4. TERMINATION AND SEVERANCE

### 4.1 Termination by the Company for Cause
The Company may terminate the Executive's employment immediately for "Cause" upon written notice to the Executive. For purposes of this Agreement, "Cause" shall mean:
1. The Executive’s material breach of this Agreement or the Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement;
2. The conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, any felony or crime of moral turpitude;
3. Fraud, embezzlement, or material dishonesty against the Company; or
4. Intentional and continuous refusal to perform the reasonable and lawful directives of the Board, which remains uncured for fifteen (15) days following written notice.
Upon termination for Cause, the Executive shall receive only his earned but unpaid Base Salary and accrued but unused PTO through the date of termination (the "Accrued Obligations"), and shall not be entitled to any severance.

### 4.2 Termination by the Company Without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason
If the Company terminates the Executive’s employment without Cause, or if the Executive resigns his employment for "Good Reason", then, subject to the Executive executing a general release of claims in favor of the Company, the Executive shall be entitled to:
1. **Severance Pay**: Continued payment of his Base Salary for a period of **twelve (12) months** following the termination date;
2. **COBRA Continuation**: Payment of the Executive’s health insurance premiums under COBRA for up to twelve (12) months; and
3. **Option Vesting**: Immediate acceleration of vesting for any unvested stock options or shares held by the Executive that would have vested during the twelve (12) month period following termination.

### 4.3 Definition of "Good Reason"
"Good Reason" shall mean the occurrence of any of the following events without the Executive's express written consent:
1. A material reduction in the Executive’s Base Salary, authority, duties, or responsibilities;
2. A relocation of the Executive's principal place of work to a location more than fifty (50) miles from Tijeras, New Mexico; or
3. A material breach of this Agreement by the Company.

---

## 5. IRC SECTION 409A COMPLIANCE
This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, **Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code** (regarding nonqualified deferred compensation). Each payment under this Agreement shall be treated as a separate payment. Any severance payments that would be paid within the first six (6) months following a separation from service of a "specified employee" (as defined under Section 409A) shall be delayed and paid on the first business day of the seventh month following such separation.

---

## 6. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

### 6.1 Dispute Resolution
Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its breach, shall be settled by confidential binding arbitration administered by the **American Arbitration Association (AAA)** in **Albuquerque, New Mexico**, under its Employment Arbitration Rules.

### 6.2 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the **State of Delaware** (corporate matters) and the **State of New Mexico** (employment matters).

### 6.3 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the executive employment of Rick Jefferson, and supersedes any prior oral or written agreements.

# PROPRIETARY INFORMATION AND INVENTIONS AGREEMENT (PIIA)

**THIS PROPRIETARY INFORMATION AND INVENTIONS AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is entered into as of June 28, 2026, by and between **Mosby Tax CRM, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and the undersigned individual (the "Service Provider" or "Employee").

### RECITALS

A. The Company is engaged in the development, marketing, and distribution of tax CRM software, financial workflow automation, and client relationship management systems.
B. The Company wishes to employ or engage the services of the Service Provider, and the Service Provider wishes to be employed or engaged by the Company.
C. In connection with such employment or engagement, the Service Provider will have access to the Company’s highly sensitive proprietary information, intellectual property, and trade secrets.
D. To protect the Company’s competitive advantages and business value, the parties desire to enter into this Agreement regarding confidentiality and the assignment of intellectual property.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the employment or engagement of the Service Provider, and the compensation paid to the Service Provider, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

### 1.1 Non-Use and Non-Disclosure
The Service Provider agrees that, both during and after their relationship with the Company, they will hold in strict confidence, and will not use (except for the sole benefit of the Company) or disclose to any third party, any Proprietary Information (as defined below) without the prior written consent of the Board of Directors of the Company.

### 1.2 Definition of Proprietary Information
"Proprietary Information" means any and all non-public, confidential, or proprietary information of the Company, including but not limited to:
1. **Technical Assets**: Source code, database structures, designs, wireframes, user interfaces, algorithms, APIs, systems architecture, security protocols, product development schedules, and testing results;
2. **Business Assets**: Customer lists, prospect logs, pricing structures, sales funnels, financial models, marketing strategies, business plans, and legal templates; and
3. **Third-Party Data**: Any tax records, taxpayer identification numbers, bank accounts, or financial statements of the Company's clients, which the Company is obligated to keep confidential.

---

## 2. INVENTIONS AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 2.1 Ownership and Assignment of Inventions
The Service Provider agrees that all inventions, discoveries, developments, concepts, designs, ideas, software code, database structures, improvements, trade secrets, trademarks, or copyrights (collectively, "Inventions") that are conceived, made, developed, or reduced to practice by the Service Provider (either alone or jointly with others) during the period of their relationship with the Company shall be the **sole and exclusive property of the Company**, provided that such Inventions:
1. Relate directly or indirectly to the business of the Company or its actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development;
2. Result from any work performed by the Service Provider for or on behalf of the Company; or
3. Are developed using any of the Company's equipment, facilities, supplies, or Proprietary Information.

### 2.2 Irrevocable Transfer
The Service Provider hereby irrevocably and unconditionally assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company all of their right, title, and interest in and to all such Inventions, including all copyrights, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, and other intellectual property rights therein.

### 2.3 Duty to Assist and Power of Attorney
The Service Provider agrees to assist the Company in every proper way, both during and after their relationship, to secure, patent, register, and enforce the Company's rights in the Inventions in any country.
- If the Company is unable, because of the Service Provider's mental or physical incapacity, or for any other reason, to secure the Service Provider's signature to apply for or pursue any patent, trademark, or copyright registration, the Service Provider hereby **irrevocably designates and appoints the Company and its duly authorized officers as their agent and attorney-in-fact**, to act for and in their behalf and stead to execute and file any such applications.

---

## 3. PRIOR INVENTIONS (EXCLUSIONS)
The Service Provider has listed in **Schedule A** attached hereto all Inventions made or conceived by them prior to their relationship with the Company (the "Prior Inventions") which they desire to exclude from the scope of this Agreement. If no such list is attached, the Service Provider represents that there are no Prior Inventions.

---

## 4. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

### 4.1 Non-Solicitation of Employees
During the term of their relationship with the Company and for a period of **twelve (12) months** following the termination of such relationship for any reason, the Service Provider shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit, or attempt to hire away any employee, independent contractor, or consultant of the Company to leave their relationship with the Company.

### 4.2 Non-Solicitation of Customers
During the term of their relationship with the Company and for a period of **twelve (12) months** following the termination of such relationship for any reason, the Service Provider shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or attempt to divert any customer, client, or active prospect of the Company with whom the Service Provider had contact or about whom they obtained Proprietary Information.

---

## 5. RETURN OF COMPANY PROPERTY
Upon termination of their relationship with the Company for any reason, or at any time upon the Company's request, the Service Provider shall immediately return to the Company all Company property, including but not limited to laptop computers, mobile devices, security keys, documents, files, notebooks, and storage media containing any Proprietary Information, and shall permanently delete any local or cloud-based copies of Company files.

---

## 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 6.1 Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the **State of Delaware**, without regard to conflict of laws principles. The exclusive jurisdiction for any action arising out of this Agreement shall be the state or federal courts in **Albuquerque, New Mexico** (or Delaware, at the Company's sole option).

### 6.2 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
